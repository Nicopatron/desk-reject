# Decisions log

What the editor already raised on your abstracts, and what you decided about it.

**You write this file. The editor can't.** In a Claude Project Claude has no file access — it ends each critique with a log line in copy-paste form, and putting it here is your move. Any claim that this logs itself would be false, and checkable in thirty seconds (R10).

**Why bother.** R4.8 says the editor never relitigates a finding you already rejected. It can only honour that by reading this file. *The specialist can't avoid what it can't see* — with an empty log, it will raise your rejected finding again on the next abstract, and it will be right to, because it has no way to know you already thought about it and said no.

Your rejections are the valuable rows. A row where you said no is you doing the job the editor exists to provoke.

## Format

```
<YYYY-MM-DD> | "<abstract short name>" | push: <one line> | rung <asserted> vs ceiling <ceiling> | <rule ids> | author: <accepted | rejected — why | deferred>
```

The `author:` field ships blank from the editor and stays blank until *you* fill it. It is your verdict on the editor, and the editor filling it in would be it grading its own work.
